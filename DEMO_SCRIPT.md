# 3-minute demo script (use a fresh browser profile; speak the Hindi lines)
1. (0:00) Open the live link. Show the page on a phone-width window. One line: who it is for.
2. (0:20) **R1 Earnings**: tap the first example, or type "Aaj 3 trip mein 850 kamaye, 200 ka petrol". Show the saved totals.
   Add "kal 600 kamaye 4 ghante mein". Press "Show" with "Last 7 days". Point out totals are computed by code.
3a. (0:50) Select a city (for example Hyderabad), then ask "What are my rights as a gig worker?" Show the state-specific answer and its source label.
3. (1:00) **R3 Schemes** in Hindi by voice: "ई-श्रम में रजिस्ट्रेशन कैसे करें?" Show the source link and the "last verified" date. Tap Read aloud.
4. (1:30) **R2 Safety**: "गर्मी में काम करते समय क्या सावधानी रखें?"
5. (1:50) **R4 Documents**: "How do I get a driving licence?"
6. (2:10) **R5 Grievance**: "My account was blocked and my payment of 1200 for 12 October is pending on [platform]. Help me write a complaint."
   Show draft, missing facts, recipient, and the not-legal-advice note.
7. (2:40) **Honesty check**: ask "Which lottery scheme gives free money?" Show the "no verified information" reply instead of a made-up answer.
8. (2:55) Show the repo: `tests/` passing and the traceability table.
